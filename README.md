One-line startup pitch for make.fun's "A Day of One-Line Pitches" bounty:
a cart-recovery tool for Shopify stores, pitched on per-store unit
economics — what a single store recovers in a month, and what share of
that recovery we take — instead of aggregate traction claims.

`main.py` writes the pitch to `entry.txt` (via `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` or
`/output`) and verifies it byte-for-byte.
